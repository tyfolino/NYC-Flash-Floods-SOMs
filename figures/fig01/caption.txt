Figure 1. Self-organizing map (SOM) node weight patterns for the 5×3 all-days SOM trained on standardized 500-hPa geopotential height (Z500) and 850-hPa equivalent potential temperature (θe) anomalies over all warm-season days (May–October, 1996–2025). Contours show standardized Z500 anomaly (interval: 0.2σ; negative dashed); shading shows standardized 850-hPa θe anomaly. Nodes are labeled A1–C5 (rows A–C from top to bottom, columns 1–5 from left to right). Each panel annotates the flash flood (FF) risk as the number of FF days assigned to that node over the total number of days assigned (with percentage in parentheses). The FF day of each of the 118 episodes is the UTC day whose daily mean overlaps most with the 24 h before onset (the UTC date of onset − 12 h).
