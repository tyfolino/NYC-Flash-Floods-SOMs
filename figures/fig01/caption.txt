Figure 1. Self-organizing map (SOM) node weight patterns for the 5×4 all-days SOM trained on standardized 500-hPa geopotential height (Z500) and 850-hPa equivalent potential temperature (θe) anomalies over all warm-season days (May–October, 1996–2025). Contours show standardized Z500 anomaly (interval: 0.2σ; negative dashed); shading shows standardized 850-hPa θe anomaly. Nodes are labeled A1–E4 (columns A–E, rows 1–4 from top-left). Each panel annotates the flash flood (FF) risk as the number of FF days assigned to that node over the total number of days assigned (with percentage in parentheses).
