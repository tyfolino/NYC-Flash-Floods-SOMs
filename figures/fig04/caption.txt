Figure 4. Stage IV radar-based precipitation composites for each evSOM node, showing the maximum hourly accumulation in the hourly accumulations ending from T−5 h to T+6 h (i.e., rain falling within ±6 h of flash flood onset), at (left) regional scale (38.5–44°N, 78–70°W) and (right) NYC scale (40.3–41.2°N, 74.8–73.4°W). Precipitation is displayed using the probability-matched mean (PMM) technique applied to hourly Stage IV accumulations. Colors follow NWS precipitation thresholds (inches); values above 4 in are shown in cyan. Open-ocean grid cells more than ~15 km from the coast are excluded before compositing because of radar artifacts. Node labels show the number of episodes with Stage IV coverage (n). Note that n may differ between panels due to incomplete Stage IV coverage.
