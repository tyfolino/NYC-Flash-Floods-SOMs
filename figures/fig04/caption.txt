Figure 4. Stage IV radar-based precipitation composites for each evSOM node at T=0, shown at (left) regional scale (38.5–44°N, 78–70°W) and (right) NYC scale (40.3–41.2°N, 74.8–73.4°W). Precipitation is displayed using the probability-matched mean (PMM) technique applied to hourly Stage IV accumulations. Colors follow NWS precipitation thresholds (inches). Node labels show the number of episodes with Stage IV coverage (n). Note that n may differ between panels due to incomplete Stage IV coverage.
