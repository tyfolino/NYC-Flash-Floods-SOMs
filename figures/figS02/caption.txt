Figure S2. Sammon mapping (nonlinear multidimensional scaling) of the 5×4 all-days SOM. Each circle represents one SOM node; fill color indicates the U-matrix value (mean Euclidean distance to neighboring nodes in weight space, a measure of inter-node dissimilarity); circle size is proportional to hit frequency (number of days assigned to that node). Gray lines connect adjacent nodes in the original SOM grid. Node labels correspond to those in Figure 1.
