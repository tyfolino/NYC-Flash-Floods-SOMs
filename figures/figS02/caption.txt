Figure S2. Sammon mapping (Sammon, 1969; a nonlinear projection that preserves small inter-node distances) of the 5×4 all-days SOM node weight vectors. Each circle represents one SOM node; fill color indicates the U-matrix value (mean Euclidean weight-space distance to the adjacent nodes in the SOM grid, a measure of inter-node dissimilarity); circle size increases linearly with hit frequency (number of days assigned to that node). Gray lines connect adjacent nodes in the original SOM grid. Node labels correspond to those in Figure 1.
