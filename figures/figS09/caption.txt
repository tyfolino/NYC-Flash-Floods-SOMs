Figure S9. Tropical cyclone (TC) associations for flash flood episodes grouped by evSOM node. (a) IBTrACS TC tracks during TC-associated episodes, colored by evSOM node. Faint lines show the full track; bold lines show the association window, the 48 hours before onset (T−48 h to T=0); filled circles mark the TC position at T=0. The dashed rectangle indicates the TC-association domain (32–54°N, 100–68°W). An episode is TC-associated if any track point of a storm that reached tropical (or subtropical) depression strength or stronger at some point in its life, including its extratropical and remnant-low stages, lies within the domain during the window. The star marks New York City. (b) Percentage of TC-associated (colored) episodes per evSOM node. Annotated fractions give TC-associated count over total node count. The dashed horizontal line indicates the overall TC-association rate across all episodes.
