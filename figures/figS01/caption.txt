Figure S1. Temporal and diurnal distributions of the 127 flash flood episodes recorded in the study domain (1996–2025). (a) Annual counts with linear trend line (dashed). (b) Monthly distribution. (c) Diurnal distribution by hour of day (Eastern Standard Time). Episodes are derived from NOAA Storm Events Database flash flood reports for New York City, deduplicated by episode identifier and UTC calendar day. All seasons are included; the 118-episode warm-season subset used in the SOM analysis is described in the main text.
