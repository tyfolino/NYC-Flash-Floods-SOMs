Figure S10. Tropical cyclone (TC) associations for flash flood episodes grouped by evSOM node. (a) IBTrACS TC tracks during TC-associated episodes, colored by evSOM node. Faint lines show the full track; bold lines show the ±48-hour window around the episode time; filled circles mark the TC position at T=0. The dashed rectangle indicates the TC-association domain (30–54°N, 100–60°W). The star marks New York City. (b) Percentage of TC-associated (colored) episodes per evSOM node. Annotated fractions give TC-associated count over total node count. The dashed horizontal line indicates the overall TC-association rate across all episodes.
