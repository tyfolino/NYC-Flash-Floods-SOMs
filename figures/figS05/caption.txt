Figure S6. Mapping of evSOM nodes to all-days SOM nodes. Each panel corresponds to one evSOM node (A1, A2, B1, B2) and shows the percentage of flash flood episodes assigned to that evSOM node that fall into each node of the 5×4 all-days SOM (columns 1–5, rows A–D). Annotated values give episode counts. Color shading indicates the percentage, with darker orange-red tones denoting a higher concentration of episodes.
