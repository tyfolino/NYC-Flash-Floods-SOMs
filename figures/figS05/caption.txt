Figure S5. Day-to-day evolution of flash flood episodes through the 5×3 all-days SOM over the 48-hour evSOM window, by evSOM node (A1, A2, B1, B2). Each panel shows the all-days SOM grid (rows A–C, columns 1–5, as in Figure 1), shaded by node flash flood risk relative to climatology; black outlines mark nodes with risk ≥ 2× (A4, A5). Arrows show moves between all-days nodes on consecutive UTC days made by ≥ 2 episodes (light blue: d−2 → d−1; dark blue: d−1 → d0; width proportional to the number of episodes); gray lines show moves made by a single episode. Red rings give the number of episodes in each all-days node on the FF day (d0; the UTC date of onset − 12 h, as in Figure 1). Because both SOMs are trained on Z500 and 850-hPa θe, the paths differ between evSOM nodes partly by construction; the figure illustrates how each evSOM archetype evolves rather than providing independent evidence for the grouping.
