Figure S8. Per-node distributions of maximum hourly precipitation within ±6 hours of flash flood episode onset. Left column: Stage IV radar-based precipitation (inches) over a domain centered on the NYC urban core (40.5–40.9°N, 74.3–73.7°W), which is smaller than the NYC-scale display extent in Fig. 4 to avoid including precipitation unrelated to NYC flash flooding. Right column: maximum hourly ASOS gauge precipitation (inches) across JFK, LGA, Central Park, and EWR. Red dashed lines indicate the median. ASOS values use UTC hour-ending times, matching the episode onset times. Node counts (n) give the episodes with available data (Stage IV coverage, or at least one ASOS station reporting).
